# Bringing Up California homepage

A modern, accessible homepage for Bringing Up California, a multiracial, multisector coalition working for California's youngest children and families.

Built around the coalition's mission to make California the best state to have, raise and be a child, with clear paths for policymakers, advocates, partners and families, and a searchable policy and resource finder.

## Design

- Light version in the coalition's official colours: teal #00A0A0, coral red #F04050, golden yellow #FFC010, leaf green #90C050 and olive grey #808060, on white
- Official Bringing Up California logo
- February Retreat (February 19-20, 2025) hero photograph and 2025 Inaugural Summit photographs
- Audience front doors for policymakers, advocates and partners, and families
- Five policy priorities, with a working policy and resource finder
- Community stories, news and events, newsletter signup and take-action band
- English / Spanish toggle
